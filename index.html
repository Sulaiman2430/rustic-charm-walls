Our Story

Art That Lives
On Your Walls

Rustic Charm Walls is a labour of love — every mandala is hand-painted with meditative focus, blending traditional Indian art forms like Meenakari with contemporary mandala geometry.

✦
100% Handmade
No prints, no shortcuts. Every stroke is painted by hand on premium MDF or canvas board.
◈
Custom Sizes & Colours
From 12" to 36" diameter. Match your wall's palette with custom color themes.
❧
Traditional Techniques
Inspired by Rajasthani Meenakari, dot mandala art, and floral motif traditions.
✿
Mirror & Gemstone Work
Select pieces feature embedded mirrors, beads, and decorative elements for extra dimension.
